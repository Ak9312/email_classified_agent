# Privacy Policy – Job Email Agent

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Job Email Agent ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to label emails in the owner's own job-search Gmail account.

## What data the app accesses

- The app accesses **only the owner's own Gmail account**, through the Gmail API, after the owner signs in with Google.
- It reads new incoming emails (sender, recipients, subject, date, a few technical headers and the message text) solely to decide which label to apply.
- It does not access any other Google account or any other Google data.

## What the app does with Gmail

- The app's **only** action in Gmail is to **add one of four labels**: Interview / Recruiter, Job Alerts, Rejections, or Other.
- It **never deletes, archives, sends, forwards or marks emails as read**, never removes labels, and never creates or changes labels.

## Sharing with an AI model

- To classify an email, the sender, subject, a few technical headers and up to the first 3,000 characters of the message text may be sent to an AI model via **OpenRouter** (openrouter.ai). This happens **only for classification**.
- Some emails are classified by simple sender rules and are not sent anywhere.
- Free AI models on OpenRouter are run by third-party providers, who may log requests under their own policies.
- Attachments are never sent.

## Storage

- The app has **no database and does not store email content**. The only thing it leaves behind is the label added in Gmail.
- The hosting provider (Vercel) keeps short-lived technical logs. These may include an email's sender address, subject and the label chosen, but never the message text. They are deleted automatically under the hosting provider's log-retention policy.
- The Google sign-in token is kept only in the app's private hosting configuration, and is used only to access the owner's own mailbox.

## Sharing and selling

- Data is not sold, shared or used for advertising, and is not transferred to anyone except as described under "Sharing with an AI model".
- The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
